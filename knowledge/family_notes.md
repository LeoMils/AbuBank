# Family Notes — soft knowledge

Freeform, human context about the family: stories, preferences, places, biographical
color. This layer is for **warm, personal answers only**. It is **NEVER** used for
relationship reasoning — all relationships come from the structured `family_graph.json`.

Each `## <name>` section (name or alias) holds that person's soft note. Leo can add,
edit, or expand sections freely; the loader picks them up on reload.
