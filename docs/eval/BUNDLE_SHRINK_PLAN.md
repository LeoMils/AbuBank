## Honest limit
The core floor is ~5886 chars: SAFETY (~1.3k, must ship every turn) and the persona
(~2.2k) dominate. Reaching <5,000 ALSO requires condensing the persona — that trades warmth
and is device-measured off/on, NOT deleted here. Even so, a typical turn drops from
13221 to ~5886-7951 chars once injection is enabled.
