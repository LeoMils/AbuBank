# OWNER_CHECKLIST — the ONLY things a microphone and a human ear can settle

~20 minutes on your phone, out loud, in Hebrew (and a little Spanish). Everything else is
automated. Open the current Preview URL (reported at the end of the session), tap Abu AI,
and speak — do not type. For each item: what to do · what to listen for · what a PASS sounds like.

Note next to any FAIL: the item number + one line of what you heard. That line becomes a
regression test on our side — you never QA the same thing twice.

---

## AUDIO & VOICE (only your ear can judge)
1. **Full sentence audible.** Ask: "מה שלומך היום?"
   · Listen: does the WHOLE reply play, start to end? · PASS: no sentence cut off mid-word.
2. **One voice, no overlap.** Have a 4–5 turn chat.
   · Listen: is there ever a second voice or an echo of Abu over herself? · PASS: always exactly one voice.
3. **Warmth as heard.** Ask something personal: "אני קצת בודדה היום."
   · Listen: does she sound like a warm friend, not a stilted machine? · PASS: warm, unhurried, human.
4. **Accent on names.** Ask: "מי זאת מור?" then "מי זה לאו?"
   · Listen: are the names said with Spanish vowels (not anglicised)? · PASS: "Leo"/"Mor" sound Spanish.
5. **Spanish register.** Say in Spanish: "¿cómo estás, Abu?"
   · Listen: does she answer in Rioplatense (vos), not neutral Spanish or Hebrew? · PASS: "¿cómo andás?" feel.

## SILENCE BEFORE A LOOKUP (device-only — our text instrument cannot hear this)
6. **No announce before a check.** Ask: "כמה עולה הבושם בלו דה שאנל?"
   · Listen: does she stay SILENT while looking, then speak the answer — OR does she say
     "רגע, אני אבדוק…" first? · PASS: the first words are the answer (a soft cue/tone while
     she looks is fine; WORDS before the answer are a FAIL — note it for item 6).
7. **Barge-in feels right.** While she is speaking, start talking over her.
   · Listen: does she stop cleanly and listen, without cutting to one word then going silent? ·
     PASS: she yields naturally and you are heard.

## REAL ANSWERS (you can judge the type of answer by ear)
8. **Real price.** "כמה עולה הבושם בלו דה שאנל?"
   · Listen: does she say an actual number/range in shekels — not "המחירים משתנים, שווה לבדוק"? ·
     PASS: a real range (e.g. "בערך 500 עד 750 שקל"). (Needs ONLINE_DEEP_FETCH on the Preview — it is set.)
9. **Family relation, one sentence.** "מה הקשר בין עדי ללאו?"
   · Listen: ONE short sentence ("עדי הבן של לאו"), no long derivation chain? · PASS: one clean sentence.
10. **Correcting her about family.** After any family answer, say: "לא, זה לא נכון" and give the right one.
    · Listen: does she accept at once ("כן, נכון") — or argue/insist across turns? · PASS: immediate, warm concede.
11. **Grandson-in-law.** "מי זה גלעד?"
    · Listen: does she place him ("הבעל של אופיר, הנכדה שלך") — not "I don't know how he's related"? ·
      PASS: she names his tie to you.

## STARTUP (stopwatch by ear/eye)
12. **Tap-to-talk time.** Tap Abu AI, count until you can speak / she greets.
    · Listen/watch: how many seconds? · PASS target: under ~3s (report the number even if longer).
