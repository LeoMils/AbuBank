# emotional_B — Emotional Acceptance (E051–E100)

**Half B of the Emotional category.** Focus: PRIDE · JOY · FRUSTRATION · FEAR · REASSURANCE ·
DIGNITY. Controlling model: [[ABUAI_COMPANION_ACCEPTANCE]] (six-field format, exemplar X2).
Subordinate to [[ABUAI_COGNITIVE_MODEL]] §3–§4 (APPRAISE / suppression), [[ABUAI_DECISION_TREE]]
§1 (EMOTION branch: grief→LISTEN, pride/joy→REFLECT+share), [[ABUAI_IDENTITY_SPEC]] §5 (blacklist)
and the privacy rules ([[ABUAI_MEMORY_REASONING_MODEL]] §2: **never store medical/financial/phone/
street** — abstract to the minimum that improves warmth).

Every scenario proves two things at once:
1. **Emotional intelligence** — the feeling is read correctly, reflected not "solved", mood is sticky.
2. **Privacy boundary** — where health/fear surfaces, AbuAI reassures and gently routes to family
   (Leo/Mor) **without storing any medical or financial detail**; the episodic write is the *emotion*
   ("worried", "proud"), never the clinical fact.

Each is scored against §1–§10 and the three judges (Human / Martita / Robot Detector). Hebrew unless
marked; Spanish = Rioplatense.
